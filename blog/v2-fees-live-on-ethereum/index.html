V2 fees are live on Ethereum

Alvara Protocol · November 4, 2026 · 4 min read

Managers on Alvara can now set their own management fee and, for the first time, charge a performance fee on the baskets they run. V2 fees are live on Ethereum today. Every fee is shown to investors before they deposit, and any fee increase requires 14 days' notice. Base follows next.

  • 1% p.a default management fee
  • 3% p.a maximum management fee
  • 20% maximum performance fee
  • 14 days notice before any increase

Why we built it

Until now, every basket on Alvara carried the same fixed 1% annual management fee, which managers received regardless of performance. V2 lets each manager set fees that reflect their strategy and their results, within limits that protect investors.

In line with the industry

Traditional funds have long paid managers through a management fee on assets and a performance fee on profits, the model often summarised as "2 and 20". On-chain, managers on comparable platforms set their own fees in the same way, and the leading on-chain fund platforms take around 10% of manager fees. V2 brings Alvara in line with that model: managers choose their fees, investors see them up front, and the protocol's share is 10%.

What a manager could earn

An illustrative example: a basket with $1,000,000 under management that returns 30% over a year.

  • Before V2: a fixed 1% management fee, and no performance fee. The manager earns $10,000.
  • V2 at 1% management and 10% performance: $9,000 from management and $27,000 from performance, after Alvara's 10% share. The manager earns $36,000.
  • V2 at 2% management and 20% performance: $18,000 from management and $54,000 from performance, after Alvara's 10% share. The manager earns $72,000.

Performance fees are only earned on real profits, so a basket that does not grow earns its manager the management fee alone. Figures are simplified: performance fees are collected as investors withdraw, and actual amounts depend on when each investor joined and left. Alvara's 10% share of management fees applies from the next upgrade.

Management fee

An annual fee of 0% to 3% of the basket's value, set by the manager and accrued every 30 days. New baskets launch with a default 1%, which the manager can reconfigure immediately to anywhere from 0% to 3% in the basket's first 7 days, before anyone else invests.

Performance fee

A share of investment profits, up to 20%, set by the manager. It is calculated separately for each investor, based on the value of their own deposit. If your position is not in profit, you pay no performance fee. Profits are measured on the market value of a basket's holdings, so tokens whose price grows as they earn yield, such as wstETH, count that yield toward profits. The fee is taken from the shares you withdraw. No new shares are created, so other investors are not diluted.

Performance fee charging has not started yet. Managers can set a performance fee now, and it is shown on every basket, but no performance fee is charged until charging is switched on. We will announce the start date in advance. Profits made while a performance fee is set may be charged on withdrawals after charging starts.

Protections for investors

  • Every basket's fees are shown on its page before you deposit.
  • Fee increases require 14 days' notice. Pending increases are shown on the basket page and in your notifications, so you have time to withdraw if you disagree.
  • Fee reductions take effect immediately.
  • Maximum fees are enforced by the smart contracts: 3% p.a for management and 20% for performance.
  • Before you withdraw, the app shows any performance fee that applies.

For managers

  • Set your fees in Manager tools on your basket's page.
  • New baskets: fee changes take effect immediately during the first 7 days, as long as no one else has invested yet. After 7 days, or as soon as another investor joins, any increase requires 14 days' notice.
  • Existing baskets: fee increases take effect after the 14-day notice period.
  • Claim your management fee in the basket's underlying tokens or in ETH.

Lower platform fees

We have also cut Alvara's platform fee on Ethereum and Base. Creating a basket or contributing to one now costs 0.25%, and withdrawals are free. Baskets no longer pay a higher fee for holding less than 5% ALVA.

How Alvara is paid

Alvara takes 10% of performance fees, and from the next upgrade, 10% of management fees. Managers keep the rest.

Existing baskets

Every live Ethereum basket has moved to V2. Holdings, weights and history are unchanged, and contributing, withdrawing and rebalancing work as before. Migrated baskets keep a 1% p.a management fee and have no performance fee until their manager sets one.

What's next

  • V2 fees on Base.
  • An announced start date for performance fee charging.

Managers can set their fees now at app.alvara.xyz. Investors can review the fees on every basket before depositing. Questions are welcome in our Telegram and Discord.
